### Notes on Acceptable Deviations

1. **2,060/2,061 m4.html, not 2,061/2,061** — parcel `1314_35_31` returns `empty_form:block_missing` from the OPRS server for every component. Direct HTTP probe confirms server-side gap, not collection bug. ROADMAP success criterion 1 says "all 2,061" but the +0.05% miss is a real-world data-gap that no engineering can close. Acceptable.

2. **2,048/2,060 taxlist_2026.pdf** — 12 block-79 parcels have m4+prc+ch75 but no 2026 taxlist row server-side (subdivisions added after the 2026 tax list was finalized). Directly probed and confirmed real-world data gap. Acceptable.

3. **owner_name → owner_mailing_address rename in modiv_history.parquet** — Plan/CONTEXT D-35 asserted Bloustein CSVs carry owner names. Direct inspection shows they do not; the closest signal is `street_address + city_state` (mailing address). Loader corrected to use `owner_mailing_address`. Spirit of D-35 (private-use-only owner-identifying data, strip on Phase 3 export) preserved with a tighter PII strip target. ROADMAP success criterion 7 does not name this column explicitly, so the rename does not invalidate the criterion.

4. **parse_taxlist_pdf signature deviation** — taxlist PDFs are borough-wide multi-parcel pages; `(path, block, lot)` is required to disambiguate the row. Output dict matches D-32 schema exactly. Documented in 01.5-04 SUMMARY.
